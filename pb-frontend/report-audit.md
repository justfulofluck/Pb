# ASVA Group On-Page SEO Audit

**Site audited:** `https://asvagroup.in/`  
**Audit type:** On-page SEO audit  
**Audit date:** March 15, 2026  
**Pages reviewed:** Homepage, About page  
**Likely target keyword set:** `construction company in Bihar`, `construction company in Siwan`, `BIM modelling`, `structural steel fabrication`, `passive fire protection`

## Executive Summary

The current site has a usable base, but the on-page SEO is underperforming because the homepage and supporting pages are not clearly optimized for a focused search intent. The biggest problems are duplicated sections, unfinished placeholder copy, weak heading strategy, weak trust signals, and insufficient keyword targeting.

**Overall on-page SEO score:** `46/100`

The site is unlikely to perform strongly for competitive commercial queries until the page structure, copy quality, and keyword focus are tightened.

## Key Findings

### 1. Weak H1 and unclear homepage focus

**Issue:**  
The homepage H1 is `ASVA Group`, which is branded but not descriptive for search engines.

**Why it matters:**  
Search engines use the H1 to understand primary page intent. A brand-only H1 wastes an opportunity to rank for service and location terms.

**Recommended fix:**  
Use a descriptive H1 such as:

- `Construction Company in Bihar for BIM, Fabrication and Civil Works`
- `ASVA Group: Construction and Engineering Services in Bihar`

### 2. Title tag inconsistency

**Issue:**  
The live homepage appears as `ASVA Group - The Best Construction Company in Bihar`, while search results also show `Home - ASVA Group`.

**Why it matters:**  
This suggests inconsistent title signals or an older title still being indexed.

**Recommended fix:**  
Standardize the homepage title to one version and keep it stable.

**Recommended title:**  
`Construction Company in Bihar | BIM, Fabrication & Civil Works | ASVA Group`

### 3. Meta description is weak, missing, or not effective

**Issue:**  
Search snippets appear to pull generic body text rather than a strong summary.

**Why it matters:**  
This usually means the meta description is missing, too generic, or unsupported by the on-page content.

**Recommended fix:**  
Add a keyword-focused meta description:

`ASVA Group is a construction company in Bihar offering BIM modelling, engineering drafting, steel fabrication, passive fire protection, plumbing and civil works. Contact us for project support.`

### 4. Duplicate sections on the homepage

**Issue:**  
`What Our Clients Say` appears more than once, and some service-related content also feels repeated.

**Why it matters:**  
Duplicate sections reduce clarity, dilute topical focus, and make the page look low quality.

**Recommended fix:**  
Remove duplicate testimonial and service blocks. Keep one clean, high-quality version of each section.

### 5. Placeholder or unfinished content

**Issue:**  
Some sections contain obvious filler or incomplete content, including service descriptions and CTA areas.

**Why it matters:**  
This damages trust, reduces perceived quality, and weakens E-E-A-T signals.

**Recommended fix:**  
Replace all placeholder text immediately. If a section is not ready, remove or hide it until complete.

### 6. Keyword targeting is too broad

**Issue:**  
The homepage mentions many services but does not strongly target one primary keyword cluster.

**Why it matters:**  
Search engines need a clear topic focus to rank the page for commercial terms.

**Recommended fix:**  
Make the homepage target one main cluster:

- `construction company in Bihar`
- `construction company in Siwan`

Use separate service pages to target narrower service keywords.

### 7. Service pages are not strongly supported from the homepage

**Issue:**  
Service references on the homepage are short and generic.

**Why it matters:**  
Internal links pass more value when the surrounding copy and anchor text are specific and relevant.

**Recommended fix:**  
Add 2 to 3 lines of unique, keyword-rich summary for each service and use descriptive anchors such as:

- `BIM modelling services`
- `Passive fire protection services`
- `Structural steel fabrication in Bihar`

### 8. Weak trust signals

**Issue:**  
Testimonials are generic and use labels like `Client 1`, `Client 2`, and `Client 3`.

**Why it matters:**  
Anonymous testimonials reduce credibility for both users and search quality evaluation.

**Recommended fix:**  
Use real client names, company names, project context, locations, and project photos where possible.

### 9. Brand inconsistency

**Issue:**  
There appears to be inconsistent spelling such as `ASVA` and `ASWA`.

**Why it matters:**  
Brand inconsistency weakens trust and entity recognition.

**Recommended fix:**  
Standardize the brand spelling across the entire site.

### 10. About page has low SEO value

**Issue:**  
The About page is mostly generic company information and does not strongly support local or service intent.

**Why it matters:**  
Important support pages should reinforce expertise, location relevance, and service authority.

**Recommended fix:**  
Add sections covering:

- Why choose ASVA Group in Bihar
- Industries served
- Project locations
- Construction and engineering capabilities
- Certifications, safety, and compliance

### 11. Local SEO signals are too weak

**Issue:**  
The site mentions `Siwan` and `Bihar`, but not strongly enough across important SEO elements.

**Why it matters:**  
Local relevance is critical if the business wants regional rankings.

**Recommended fix:**  
Reinforce local intent in:

- Title tag
- H1
- Intro paragraph
- Contact section
- Footer

Also create a strong contact page with full NAP details and service area coverage.

### 12. Weak conversion structure

**Issue:**  
The site includes contact information, but CTAs are not structured strongly enough.

**Why it matters:**  
SEO pages should turn traffic into leads with clear next actions.

**Recommended fix:**  
Use stronger CTAs such as:

- `Request a Quote`
- `Talk to Our Team`
- `Get BIM / Fabrication Support`

Place a short inquiry form near the top of the homepage.

## Priority Fixes

### Critical

- Remove duplicate sections from the homepage
- Remove all placeholder or incomplete content
- Standardize brand spelling across the site

### High Priority

- Rewrite homepage title, meta description, and H1 around one primary keyword
- Improve service summaries with descriptive keyword targeting
- Add real testimonials and proof elements
- Strengthen local SEO signals for `Siwan, Bihar`

### Medium Priority

- Improve About page content for expertise and location relevance
- Strengthen internal linking to service pages
- Improve CTA structure for lead generation
